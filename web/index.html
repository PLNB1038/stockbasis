The scanner walks the wallet's recent history until it collects ~30 stock trades (or hits its budget), classifies tokenized-equity mints by on-chain token tags (xStocks, Sunrise, Ondo, PreStocks) and computes FIFO cost basis in USD. WSOL cash legs are valued at the SOL price of the trade day; network fees are excluded from trade values. Wash-sale adjustments are not applied — treat the output as a preliminary statement and consult a tax professional. Read-only: the tool never asks for keys and never moves funds.
